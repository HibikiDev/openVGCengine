Open team sheets work as they are. Your field's teams need no EVs: paste what the sheet shows, and each set without a spread gets its species' most used one from the usage stats. A nature, if the paste has one, is kept.
How to write the teams
  • One {% if has_library %}library id (BR-111, NI-1, LO-25) or {% endif %}pokepast.es link per line: each line is one team.
  • For a pasted Showdown export, separate teams with a line of ---.
  • A weight goes at the end of a line or a header: {{ 'BR-111' if has_library else 'https://pokepast.es/0123abcd' }} x2 means faced twice as often as a team at x1. No weight means x1.
  • An optional header line # Label x3 above a team names it and sets its weight.
  • pokepast.es links are read once, when you save. The bucket keeps the team itself, so later edits on pokepast don't change it.
  • A bucket holds one format's teams; the tools offer it in that format.
  • Spreads are optional. A set without EVs (stat points in Reg M-C) gets its species' most used spread from the usage stats, under its nature if the paste shows one, so an open team sheet is all a team needs. A set with its own spread keeps it, even next to sets without one. Real spreads are more exact when you know them.
  • Every team is checked when you save. If any team fails, nothing is saved and the problems are listed.
