How luck is measured

Each chance roll is replayed both ways - the chance event and the usual outcome - and the gap in the model's win estimate, times the event's chance, is its worth per game. Chance events: misses, crits, added effects that fire, confusion, full paralysis, short sleeps, thaws, Quick Claw; guaranteed effects don't count. "Creates" / "carries" are your own Pokémon's rolls; damage rolls are shown as a spread.
