How this works

The bot pilots both teams, thinking every turn through the way it plays a real game (Search Mode), so this measures how well the bot plays your team - one that needs a human touch may rate lower than it deserves. Each team's score is good to about ±{{ (98 / 48 ** 0.5)|round|int }} points at 48 games. The bring matrix then plays your likeliest fours against theirs, plus all 15 fours of each side screened for answers: both adapt is your score when both sides pick their brings well.

Fast mode plays {{ FAST_FACTOR }}× the games on instinct, without search or bring matrix: good for ranking teams, but a single matchup can be ~20 points off the searched bot.
