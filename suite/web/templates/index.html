Study VGC with a model that learned it from scratch

openVGCengine's model taught itself VGC through over a billion self-play games. These tools let you ask it things: about your replays, your team, your next team preview.

Its opinions are its own view, not a verdict: at a high level a "worse" play is often a read, and the model isn't perfect either.
